Overhead Bin

1. Tap a bag, tap again to rotate, then drag it over a bin (or tap a column). Bags fall like Tetris.

2. A full bin latches for a bonus and you move aft to the next one. The cabin only has so many bins.

3. Don't let the aisle back up to the door. Gate-check a bag that fits nowhere.
